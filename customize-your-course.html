Davina could simply be forgetful – or she could be a criminal who wants to hide stolen cash. The only way to be certain is to understand who Davina really is.

CIP processes are designed to help firms like Haru’s confirm the identities of clients like Davina and manage any risks associated with doing business with them. The CIP is one important step toward protecting ourselves and our community from the danger of financial crimes. That’s why it’s important to make sure that every step of the process is followed correctly, and all the necessary documentation is collected.

Even when you are busy, or under pressure from a customer or superior, it’s vital that you take the time to collect all the information and documentation that we need. We count on you to follow our CIP processes and to let us know if you ever identify any shortcomings in our system.
